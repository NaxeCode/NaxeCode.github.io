Schedule Rehearsal

The C# application, scenario manifests, replay harness, visitor interface, and original
documentation were created for this project. JSON receipts are output of its authored
scenarios. No private schedules or customer records are included.

Quartz.NET 4.8.1 executes the jobs and provisions its own SQLite schema.
Noda Time 3.3.7 resolves the two time-zone rule sets. Microsoft.Data.Sqlite and
Microsoft.Extensions.TimeProvider.Testing provide the database driver and test clock.
These dependencies are restored from NuGet with package hashes in src/packages.lock.json.
They are not bundled in the static website.

The three files under data/ are unmodified Noda Time NZD distributions:
https://nodatime.org/tzdb/tzdb2026d.nzd
https://nodatime.org/tzdb/tzdb2026e.nzd
https://nodatime.org/tzdb/tzdb2026f.nzd
They contain IANA time-zone rules and Unicode CLDR mappings. See licenses/ for upstream
notices and license texts. Each file's SHA-256 is validated at runtime.
https://data.iana.org/time-zones/
https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr
https://github.com/nodatime/nodatime
https://github.com/quartznet/quartznet
