Schedule Rehearsal

Original visitor interface and authored scenario receipts. The static presentation
replays measurements recorded by a C# CLI using Quartz.NET 4.8.1, SQLite, Noda Time
3.3.7, and Microsoft.Extensions.TimeProvider.Testing. The site contains no bundled
scheduler binaries, zone databases, third-party fonts, or external images.

IANA releases 2026e and 2026f supply the underlying rules.
https://data.iana.org/time-zones/
https://nodatime.org/tzdb/
https://github.com/quartznet/quartznet
